# TIme-test

## Time-test is a simple Python console application that tests your math skills. Train your brain and speed of your calculations with it!



## How does the application work?

    1. The application generates 10 random math problems, 
        every type, one by one. 

    2. Every time has a time limit to 7 seconds. 
        So you'd better be quick in calculating the answer. 
        After all, your score depends on it.

    3. if every question is answered correctly and in time, 
        your score value will be increased by 1. 
        If the answer is incorrect OR the time is up, 
        you won't score any points.

    4. No matter did you answer the question correctly or not, 
        if the time is up no points will be added to your score.

    5. In the end, the application will show you your score and
         the time you spent on answering all questions.


    PS: The application is ready but it's open to improvements.If you have a concept of improvement, or a bug to report, please do so. Your feedback may change the application for the better.

    Have fun and train your brain!
